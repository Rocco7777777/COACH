Lokacija

Lokacija se koristi samo za snimanje GPS trčanja koje sportaš sam pokrene. Dok snimanje traje, aplikacija prikazuje stalnu obavijest (foreground service) kako bi snimanje radilo i dok je zaslon zaključan. Kad sportaš zaustavi trčanje, praćenje lokacije prestaje. Lokacija se ne prikuplja u pozadini u bilo koje drugo vrijeme.

Tko vidi tvoje podatke

  • Ti i trener tvog tima. Sportaši drugih timova i drugi treneri ne vide tvoje podatke (to osigurava baza podataka pravilima pristupa po timu).
  • Trener može izvesti podatke svog tima u Excel datoteku i sam je podijeliti; za to odgovara trener.

S kim dijelimo podatke (izvršitelji obrade)

  • Supabase Inc.: pohrana baze podataka i prijava korisnika (pravila Supabasea).
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation: tijekom GPS trčanja aplikacija preuzima dijelove karte s poslužitelja OpenStreetMap. Oni pritom vide IP adresu uređaja i koji dio karte se traži, ali ne i tvoje ime ni rezultate (pravila OSMF-a).
  • Google: distribucija aplikacije putem Google Playa, prema Googleovim pravilima.

Podatke ne prodajemo i ne koristimo ih za oglašavanje. Svi prijenosi idu šifrirano (HTTPS).

Pravna osnova

Izvršenje usluge koju si zatražio otvaranjem računa (čl. 6. st. 1. b GDPR-a), a za podatke o zdravlju i lokaciji tvoja izričita privola (čl. 9. st. 2. a i čl. 6. st. 1. a), koju možeš povući u bilo kojem trenutku brisanjem tih podataka ili računa.

Koliko dugo čuvamo podatke

Dok imaš račun. Nakon zahtjeva za brisanje račun i svi povezani podaci brišu se u roku od 30 dana. Lokalna kopija na telefonu briše se odjavom ili deinstalacijom aplikacije.

Tvoja prava

Imaš pravo na pristup, ispravak, brisanje, ograničenje obrade, prenosivost podataka i prigovor. Piši na [KONTAKT E-MAIL]. Imaš i pravo podnijeti pritužbu Agenciji za zaštitu osobnih podataka (azop.hr).

Brisanje računa: upute ovdje.

Djeca

Aplikacija nije namijenjena djeci mlađoj od 16 godina bez suglasnosti roditelja ili skrbnika.

Promjene

Privacy Policy: COACH

COACH app (Android, package hr.coach.app) · Effective 8 October 2026.

Controller

[IME I PREZIME], Croatia. Contact: [KONTAKT E-MAIL].

Data we collect

  • Account: name, email, password (stored only as a hash), role (coach or athlete), team.
  • Training: workouts created by the coach and results entered by the athlete: split times, gym sets (kg, reps, RPE), manually entered past records.
  • GPS runs: precise location and route, distance, time and pace, only while the athlete records a run.
  • Daily log: hours of sleep, supplements (protein, creatine, vitamins) and answers to the coach's morning wellness questionnaire. This may be health data; it is entered voluntarily.

No contacts, photos, microphone, advertising IDs or app usage data. No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.

Location

Used only to record a GPS run the athlete starts. While recording, a persistent notification (foreground service) keeps tracking working with the screen locked. Tracking stops when the run is stopped. No location is collected at any other time.

Who can see your data

You and the coach of your team only, enforced by per-team access rules in the database. A coach can export their team's data to an Excel file and is responsible for any sharing of it.

Processors

  • Supabase Inc.: database hosting and authentication.
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation: map tiles are downloaded during a GPS run; OSM sees the device's IP address and the requested map area, not your name or results.
  • Google: app distribution via Google Play.

We do not sell data or use it for advertising. All transfers are encrypted (HTTPS).

Legal basis

Performance of the service you requested (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)); explicit consent for health and location data (Art. 9(2)(a), Art. 6(1)(a)), withdrawable at any time by deleting that data or your account.

Retention

As long as you have an account. After a deletion request, the account and all related data are deleted within 30 days. The local copy on the phone is removed by logging out or uninstalling.

Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection: write to [KONTAKT E-MAIL]. You may also complain to the Croatian data protection authority (AZOP). Account deletion: instructions.

Children

Not intended for children under 16 without parental consent.
